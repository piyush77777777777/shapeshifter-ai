Core Capabilities

Designed for Instant 3D Synthesis

Experience deterministic client-side geometry extraction paired with semantic prompt color mapping. No heavy software, no GPU waiting queues.

Dual-Pass Spatial Distance Field

Converts freehand outlines into high-precision distance-from-edge topological fields, creating smooth volumetric curved surfaces with zero polygon tearing.

Semantic Natural Language Colors

Describe your asset naturally ("red roof", "wooden door", "blue armor", "golden lamp"). The NLP parser extracts material accents and paints vertex geometry on the fly.

Parametric Sculpting Controls

Tweak physical model thickness, laplacian smoothing passes, and style modes (Puffy organic, Flat mechanical, or Sharp faceted) with instant 60fps mesh updates.

Production OBJ & STL Export

One-click direct export to industry-standard .OBJ files with embedded RGB vertex colors and watertight .STL files verified for direct 3D printing slicing.

Full 360° Real-Time Orbit & Wireframe

Inspect every polygon with smooth mouse orbit, pinch zoom on touch screens, auto-rotate turntable view, and transparent wireframe debugging overlays.

100% In-Browser Privacy

All computational geometry runs locally inside your browser via WebGL and WebAssembly standards. No sketches or private design concepts ever leave your device.

Architecture & Flow

The 5-Stage Synthesis Pipeline

ShapeShifter AI translates your 2D stroke raster into a watertight 3D mesh through five coordinated algorithmic steps.

1

Draw / Upload

480x480 raster canvas captures dark stroke boundaries or imported sketches.

2

Text Prompt

NLP analyzer scans for color words (red, blue, wood, gold) and target anatomy.

3

Shape Analysis

Morphological dilation & exterior queue flood-fill isolate watertight bodies.

4

Image-to-3D

Distance transform builds front/back height fields with quad side walls.

5

Preview & Export

Three.js WebGL lighting, orbit inspection, and instant OBJ/STL file export.
